World-Class Laboratories & Facilities

High-performance hardware, GPU clusters, dedicated cloud development setups, and collaborative incubation spaces to support practical learning.

Technical Infrastructure

Specialized Computing Laboratories

Equipped with enterprise software suites, high-speed fiber internet, and 100% power redundancy.

Lab 01

Advanced Software Engineering Lab

Dedicated to full-stack web development, enterprise software architecture, and collaborative agile engineering sprints.

Capacity: 60 Workstations
Configuration: Intel Core i7 13th Gen, 32GB RAM, 1TB NVMe SSD
Software Suite: VS Code, IntelliJ IDEA, Docker, Git, MySQL, Postman
1Gbps Fiber Connected
Lab 02

AI & High Performance Computing Lab

State-of-the-art laboratory equipped with dedicated GPU acceleration for training deep learning models, computer vision, and neural network simulations.

Capacity: 45 High-Performance Systems
Configuration: NVIDIA RTX 4080 16GB GPUs, Core i9, 64GB DDR5
Software Suite: PyTorch, TensorFlow, CUDA Toolkit, JupyterLab, Anaconda
GPU Acceleration Cluster
Lab 03

IoT & Embedded Systems Lab

Specialized maker space where students design smart connected devices, sensor networks, robotics prototypes, and hardware controllers.

Capacity: 40 Student Workbenches
Hardware Kits: Raspberry Pi 5, Arduino Mega, ESP32, Digital Oscilloscopes
Software Suite: Arduino IDE, PlatformIO, Keil uVision, Proteus
Hardware Prototyping
Resource Hub

Departmental Digital Library

A comprehensive sanctuary featuring over 8,000 physical computer science textbooks, journals, and unlimited access to top international research portals.

Seating: 100 Students Reading Space
Digital Access: 20 High-Speed Online Terminals
Subscriptions: IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, Springer Nature
E-Journal Access
Auditorium

Smart Multimedia Seminar Hall

Acoustically treated interactive seminar hall hosting guest lectures by industry leaders, national hackathons, technical conferences, and student project vivas.

Capacity: 180 Seating Capacity
AV System: 4K Laser Projector, Bose Acoustics, Wireless Mics
Broadcasting: Live Streaming & Hybrid Conference Setup
Audio-Visual Theatre
Innovation

Project Incubation Cell

An open, collaborative co-working environment for student entrepreneurs and innovators to develop proof-of-concept projects and launch technology startups.

Capacity: 35 Co-working Desks
Facilities: Smart Boards, Private Conference Pods
Cloud Support: AWS / Google Cloud Credits Support
Startup Launchpad

Laboratory Safety & Usage Guidelines

01. All students must display their official college ID badge and sign into the laboratory digital registry before logging into workstations.
02. Use of external unauthorized USB storage devices is restricted to prevent malware introduction; git repositories and institutional cloud storage are standard.
03. Food and beverages are strictly prohibited inside all computational and hardware laboratories at all times.
04. GPU clusters in Lab 02 require prior reservation through the lab instructor for batch training of neural network models.
