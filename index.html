From the Head of Department

"Bridging Academic Rigor with Industry-Ready Technology Innovations"

Welcome to the Department of Computer Applications. Our Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) program has been meticulously crafted to address the rapidly evolving frontiers of software engineering, artificial intelligence, cloud architectures, and cybersecurity.

Our mission is not merely to impart conceptual literacy, but to cultivate proactive builders, problem solvers, and ethical engineers who make meaningful contributions to the global IT ecosystem through hands-on laboratory immersions, hackathons, and industry mentorship.

Dr. Rajesh Kumar Sharma

Head of Department & Professor, Ph.D. (Computer Applications)

Specialization: Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence & Data Science
