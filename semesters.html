Marking & Evaluation Breakdown

Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA) 30% Marks

Includes two centralized mid-term sessional examinations, code lab quizzes, project assignments, and attendance regularity.

End-Semester University Exam (ESE) 70% Marks

Comprehensive theoretical examination and external practical laboratory viva conducted by university-appointed examiners.
