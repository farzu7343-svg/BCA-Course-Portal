Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common queries regarding the BCA curriculum, admissions, and careers.

What is the basic eligibility for admission into the BCA program?

Candidates must have successfully completed 10+2 (Higher Secondary) with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks (45% for reserved categories). Preference is given to applicants with Mathematics or Computer Science at the senior secondary level.

What career opportunities are available after completing BCA?

Graduates can work as Full-Stack Developers, Cloud Associates, Data Analysts, Mobile App Developers, and System Administrators. Furthermore, graduates can pursue higher studies like MCA, M.Tech, or Master of Science in CS/AI.

Are practical laboratory sessions mandatory in all semesters?

Yes, each semester features dedicated core practical laboratory sessions (C lab, C++ & Data Structures, SQL & DBMS, Java, Web Development, and Mobile App Development) ensuring extensive hands-on code development.

Does the department provide placement assistance and mock interview preparation?

Absolutely. The Department Training & Placement Cell conducts competitive coding bootcamps, technical interview simulations, resume writing sessions, and hosts over 70 hiring partners on-campus each academic year.
